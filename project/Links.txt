 QUICK START FOR THE EXAMINER
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open the Live Application URL in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or
   Safari. Nothing needs to be installed.

2. Follow the 17-step walkthrough in README.pdf section 4. It
   takes about fifteen minutes and exercises every role, every
   department handover and every safety rule.

3. To change role, sign out at the bottom of the sidebar and
   sign in as another role.

4. To restore the original seeded data at any point, select
   "Reset demo data" on the login screen.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------

- The deployment loads with 28 synthetic patients, 9 staff
  accounts, 6 wards with 34 beds, and open orders, invoices and
  claims, so every screen has content on first visit.

- No real patient data is used anywhere in the system.

- The six AI features call a live service and require an internet
  connection. If one reports itself unavailable, the connection is
  down; every other function continues to work. This fallback is
  the designed behaviour and is covered by test case TC-70.

- Blocked actions are the system working as specified: a refused
  prescription, a struck-through appointment slot, a locked
  clinical tab for a non-clinical role, and a refused signature on
  a consultation with no diagnosis are all enforced rules.

- Mobile Money settlement is modelled. The provider request and
  approval sequence is faithful and the records it produces are
  correct, but no real money moves.

- Known limitations are stated in full in README.pdf section 8 and
  Project_Documentation.pdf section 20.
